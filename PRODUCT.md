# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users
- Primary: High-school students and test candidates preparing for competitive STEM exams, seeking a motivational, interactive, and personalized study companion.
- Secondary: Educators, tutors, or administrators looking to track user diagnostics and support candidate progress.

## Product Purpose
To empower students in their STEM exam preparation journey by providing low-friction mock test generation, responsive dashboard analytics, and real-time AI tutor coaching. Success is measured by sustained study engagement, reduced exam anxiety, and measurable improvement in subject-wise accuracy and speed.

## Positioning
A high-energy, gamified learning companion that converts static, intimidating mock-testing data into a motivational progression system. Unlike rigid test prep engines, it combines deep desktop-grade analytical tools with hyper-convenient mobile micro-interactions and instant chat coaching.

## Operating Context
- Environments: Late-night study desks, quick mobile review sessions during daily commutes, and timed mock test trials under high pressure.
- Devices: Fluidly adapts between desktop viewports (ideal for deep review and charts) and native-like mobile viewports (ideal for quick micro-tests and chat coaching).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Fully customizable STEM mock-test generation.
- Real-time diagnostic performance metrics (accuracy, time management, subject coverage).
- Interactive, AI-driven study buddy/chat tutor.
- Gamified milestone/reward feedback loop to incentivize daily practice.
- Constrained by standardized exam structures and scientific notation/formula presentation requirements.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: LearningBuddy (or Learning Buddy)
- Tone: Gamified & Energetic, highly encouraging, high energy, reward-centric, and motivational.
- Aesthetics: High-fidelity, vibrant accents, lively progress indicators, and welcoming, friendly tutor interactions.

## Evidence on Hand
- A modular, runnable Angular 21 + Ionic 8 application scaffold with structured dashboard diagnostics, test generation, and chat tutor frameworks.
- No external client-provided visual media or testimonial assets exist yet; keep placeholders energetic and functional.

## Product Principles
1. **Engage the Grind**: Turn repetitive test practice into a motivating progress journey using gamified, rewarding mechanics.
2. **Frictionless Mocking**: Starting, pausing, or analyzing a mock test must take minimal steps to keep the user focused on learning.
3. **Conversational Clarity**: AI tutor chat coaching must be highly encouraging, instant, and skilled at breaking down complex concepts simply.
4. **Device-Optimized Ergonomics**: Deliver robust analytics on desktop while maintaining focused, native-like touch interfaces on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Clear typography and legible layouts for complex mathematical and scientific notation.
- Robust color contrast for prolonged screen exposure during late-night study sessions.
- Clear semantic markers for keyboard-driven navigation in mock test sims.
